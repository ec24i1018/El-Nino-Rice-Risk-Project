Chicken Commander

Build your flock, study the fights, and figure out how many chickens it usually takes to defeat the T. rex.

How to play · your mission

Every T. rex has a hidden average. Across many battles, there is a typical number of chickens needed to defeat it. The exact number changes from fight to fight, but the hidden average stays the same for your entire game. Your mission is to estimate that average as accurately as possible.

You are the flock commander. You begin with 100 gold. First, buy 2–4 scout fights for 4 gold each. Scouts reveal the exact number of chickens that won those battles, giving you your first clues. Then play 10 battle rounds: choose how many chickens to send. After each battle, you learn how far your guess was from the actual number, but not whether you guessed too high or too low. You then choose between the two possible values and add your choice to the data.

Gold, risk and rewards: every chicken your guess is away from the actual requirement costs 0.5 gold—there is no per-round penalty limit. If your gold runs out, the game ends. At the end, submit your estimate of the hidden average. You earn up to 200 bonus gold, losing 40 bonus gold for every chicken your estimate is off. Your final score is your remaining gold plus this accuracy bonus.

Use the evidence: inspect the data table and histogram. You can change earlier picks and untick any observation you do not want included in your mean. Use the sample mean, standard deviation, standard error and 95% confidence interval to help judge how close your estimate might be.

Stuck? Strategy hint

A guess far from your current mean can make one of the two possible values more believable, helping you learn the direction of the hidden average. But larger misses cost more gold. Balance information gained against the cost of the guess.
